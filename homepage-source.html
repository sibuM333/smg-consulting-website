Why SMG Tutoring exists

After matric, I rewrote Mathematics and Physical Sciences, certain that more studying would fix everything.

It didn’t.

I worked hard. I studied consistently. I even changed my exam strategy mid-way through the exams themselves — and still, the problem was never effort. It was understanding.

There’s a difference between knowing information and understanding what a question is actually asking. Most learners believe they understand a concept until an exam presents it differently, and the marks reveal a gap they didn’t know was there.

That realisation is the whole foundation of SMG Tutoring.

“Many learners do not lack intelligence. They lack understanding.”

Matric Rewrite Programme

Matric rewrite candidates carry a different kind of pressure. They’ve already sat these exams once, and the weight of a previous result can be as heavy an obstacle as the content itself.

The programme focuses on confidence rebuilding, targeted exam technique, closing specific gaps rather than re-teaching everything, structured revision planning, and consistent past-paper practice under real exam conditions.

The goal isn’t to repeat the exam. It’s to walk in prepared differently.

Frequently asked questions

How are lessons conducted?

Every lesson moves through the same structure — diagnosis, explanation, guided practice, and review — matched to what the learner needs that week.

Are notes provided?

Yes. Learners receive explanations, worked examples, corrections, and revision material relevant to what was covered each session.

Is independent revision expected?

Yes. Lessons build understanding and technique, but consistent revision between sessions is what makes it stick — guidance on structuring that time is included.

Do you guarantee results?

No responsible tutor can guarantee a specific mark, and it’s worth being cautious of anyone who does. What’s guaranteed is a structured method, consistent effort, and honest feedback on progress.

What’s the payment policy?

A 50% deposit is required before lessons begin, with the balance payable on an agreed instalment plan. Lessons are confirmed once proof of payment is received.

What if a lesson needs to be rescheduled?

More than 24 hours’ notice — rescheduled at no cost. Less than 24 hours’ notice, or a missed lesson without notice, carries a 50% fee. Genuine emergencies are considered case by case.
